# STARFIGHTER — One-Page Concept (v1.1, approved)

**Pitch:** Ace Combat's squadron and missiles, Rayforce's multi-lock and 1942's arcade punch, in a free-flying top-down roguelite where every power-up is a pilot you command.

**Core fantasy:** You're an elite ace facing impossible odds. Your skill keeps you alive, but only the squadron you build and lead can win the war.

**Genre & platform:** Top-down 2D space dogfighting roguelite with free flight in all directions. Browser first and built for a controller; later PC/Steam Deck, console and mobile. A 3D spin-off is possible later.

## The hook

1. **Power-ups are pilots.** Named wingmen join you during a run, fly at your side, grow stronger, and can be shot down.
2. **Paint & strike.** Hold enemies in your nose cone to lock them; your squadron launches the missiles. More pilots means more locks and bigger salvos.
3. **Formation orders.** One-button, real-time commands (tight / spread / attack my target) add a tactical layer on top of the dogfighting.

## Design pillars

1. **Ace in the cockpit.** Your flying and aiming always decide the fight.
2. **Every pilot matters.** Wingmen are people, and losing them hurts.
3. **Fast tactics, no pauses.** Real-time orders and a battlefield readable at a glance.

## How it plays

- **Flight:** plane-like handling in space (Star Wars / Ace Combat). You fire where your nose points. Throttle and acceleration; a flight model that is easy to pick up and deep to master (slowing down to turn tighter is to be explored).
- **Camera:** zooms out as you speed up and in as you slow down. It also **looks ahead**: your ship isn't locked to the center of the screen; the camera shifts so you see more of the space in front of you. Radar and edge-of-screen indicators show enemies off screen.
- **Combat:** guns along the nose, plus lock-on missiles: keep an enemy in the cone in front of you for a moment to lock it, then fire.
- **A run:** you start alone and outnumbered. Pilots join your squadron as the run goes on, your salvos grow, and your orders matter more, building to a desperate set-piece battle against overwhelming forces. Every run is different, and some progress carries over between runs.

## Setting, tone & look

- **Setting:** space sci-fi war with starfighters, carriers, capital ships and a wide variety of enemies, fought across varied locations (nebulae, debris fields, fleet battles).
- **Tone:** grounded military drama with radio chatter and real stakes. The story comes out of each run: who joined, who fell, the comeback.
- **Look:** flat, stylized silhouettes with a limited palette. Ships are recognizable by shape, and colors code faction and threat, so it reads at any zoom on small screens.

## Built by AI agents

Clear written specs, modular and data-driven content (ships, enemies, pilots as data), automated tests, and game logic separate from rendering, so the jump to other platforms or 3D stays possible.
