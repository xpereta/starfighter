# render/spectacle

- **Purpose:** the render-only "more spectacular" layer of a style pack. A pack opts in with an optional `spectacle` section in its `StyleInput` (contract and validation: `../spectacle-contract.ts`; every sub-section is optional, complete when present, and a pack inherits sections from its parent). Packs without it (`plain`, `anime-80s`, `realistic`) are drawn exactly as before. The shipped user is `data/styles/anime-spectacle/` (parent `anime-80s`), selected with `?style=anime-spectacle` (`&dev` for the panel).
- **Reads:** the active style (`activeStyle().spectacle`), world state and the events of each step. **Writes:** nothing in core. Own seeded random streams only; the replay hash is identical under every style (`tests/sim/style.sim.test.ts` runs the effects over every step).
- **Quality:** `?fx=low|medium|high` (high by default; remembered when set in the panel's Spectacle section). Presets in `data/spectacle-quality.ts` (pool caps, post on/off, bloom resolution, MSAA). `low` is the fallback: no post-processing, no shader clouds, small pools. All effects live in fixed pools with hard caps.
- **Post-processing (`post.ts`, `post-state.ts`):** an `EffectComposer` (render, `UnrealBloomPass`, one finishing shader pass for colour fringe, vignette, scanlines, grain and the zoom punch, `OutputPass`). The finishing pass runs in linear colour so the picture matches the plain render. It falls back to the plain render without console errors when the GPU lacks float render targets, when the composer throws, when `?fx=low`, or when the panel switches it off. The transient part (hit pulse, punch) is pure and tested (`post-state.ts`).
- **Backdrop (`backdrop.ts`, `backdrop-logic.ts`):** gas-cloud shader layers (fbm noise, banded into cel steps, parallax by shifting the noise coordinates), up to three twinkling star layers, one far structure per battle's palette (a cel-shaded ringed planet, a colony ring or a carrier, sized and placed by view fractions like a matte painting, with tiny parallax), drifting rocks (two instanced polygons: body and lit rim) and distant battle flashes (busier with enemies alive and recent kills). Things that fill the view wrap into a box about the view size, so density does not depend on zoom. One palette per battle (`world.run.battle`, cycling; practice uses the first) eased over `shift` seconds; the sky colour replaces the scene background while it is on. `?sky=1..4` or the panel previews a sky. Own seeded stream, fixed pools, quality-capped.
- **Ships (`ship-fx.ts`, `ribbons.ts`):** engine plumes at each ship's glow points (outer flame, white-hot core, pulsing heat halo; length follows speed and throttle, flickers), nav lights at the wingtips found from the silhouette (port/starboard colours, a white tail strobe), wingtip trails with a faint swept sheet between them (from a speed threshold), small trails for wingmen and enemy fighters, a double-helix streak behind a rolling player, missile smoke strands that spiral around the path and widen as they fade (alpha-blended, the Macross swarm look), launch flashes and lock-on brackets that snap in on `LockAcquired`. Trails are `ribbons.ts`: a fixed pool of emitters keyed by stable ids (a missile's `uid`), rings of points that age out; missiles and the player push first so they win slots when the pool is full.
- **Combat (`combat-fx.ts`, `demo.ts`):** per kind of kill (`combat.recipes`: fighter, drone, turret, static, wingman, missile, hit) a flash frame with a four-point glint, shock rings, a banded fireball (soft glow, two hard cel layers), sparks, alpha-blended smoke ink-blots, twinkling debris glints, and a chain reaction of delayed little blasts whose last one is bigger (turrets are capital-scale); hit sparks thrown back along the shot, missile impacts, damage rings; zoom punch and colour-fringe pulses through hooks to the post pass; glowing bullet tracers and enemy plasma orbs. Layered over the style's own explosions and death pieces (which are unchanged). Pools: elements, sparks, blots and the chain queue, all capped per level. The panel's Demo blasts button (`globalThis.__sfDemo`) feeds synthetic events to the effects only. Honest limits: no real decals on hulls (sparks and rings only).
- **Glow batch (`glow-batch.ts`):** the shared drawing surface of the effects: one dynamic mesh with per-vertex RGBA, additive or alpha blended, fixed vertex budget, no allocation (discs, rings, tapered streaks, four-point stars). Values above 1 feed the bloom.
- **Panel:** the Spectacle section (`src/dev/panel-spectacle.ts`): quality level, a switch per effect group, overall intensity, and a slider for every number of the pack's spectacle (`panel-spectacle-logic.ts`). Edits change the resolved pack in memory only.
- **Test:** `spectacle-contract.test.ts` (validation, inheritance, no write-through), `post-state.test.ts` (pulse, punch, neutral, quality levels), `backdrop.test.ts` (palette easing, wrapping, pool caps per level, headless parts), `ribbons.test.ts`, `combat-fx.test.ts` (caps under a storm, hooks, demo), `ship-fx.test.ts` (caps per level, wingtips, switches), `panel-spectacle-logic.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/spectacle.spec.ts` (loads at every level without console errors, panel section), plus the generic style checks over every pack.
