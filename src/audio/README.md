# audio

- **Purpose:** plays the active style pack's sound table (`data/styles/<id>/sounds.ts`) from the game events. Presentation only: it reads events and state, never writes the world, and is never imported by `src/core` (lint enforces it). Replays and the state hash are unchanged (`tests/sim/audio.sim.test.ts` checks the hash with audio on and off, under every style).
- **Parts:**
  - `planner.ts` (pure): event -> `PlayRequest`. Looks the event up in the table, applies the minimum gap and the voice limit, rolls the pitch (`pitch` +/- `pitchRandom`, its own random stream, never the simulation's), and works out volume and pan from the event position relative to the player (`spatial`: pan and distance fade; `size`: bigger kills sound deeper and louder; `duck`: dip the music). Pitch is clamped to 0.1..10 and volume and pan to their ranges.
  - `engine.ts`: the state around it: unlocked or not, paused, muted, the mix. Events before the first key press or click are dropped (browser rule). Pause plays the `Paused` sound and suspends the effects; resume undoes it. Mute sends master 0 and keeps the volumes.
  - `backend.ts`: the interface to the hardware. `webaudio.ts` is the Web Audio one (oscillators and looped noise with an envelope, a sweep and a filter per layer, or a sample file); `fake-backend.ts` records requests for tests.
  - `index.ts`: browser wiring (`startAudio()`): the unlock on the first key or pointer press, the **M** mute key (remembered in this browser), `<html data-audio-muted>` for e2e.
  - `samples.ts`: finds sample files in `data/styles/<id>/assets/` or `data/audio/`.
- **Mix parameters:** `data/audio/mix.ts` (master, effects, music: default, range, unit, note), edited live in the panel.
- **Recipes:** a pack's `sounds.ts` is a table event -> sound. A synthesised sound is a list of layers (oscillator or noise, start and end frequency, attack and decay, loudness, optional filter with its own sweep); `data/audio/recipes.ts` has tiny helpers (`tone`, `noise`, `sfx`) that build those plain objects. `plain` has simple distinct beeps and bursts; `anime-80s` a richer set (layered explosions with delayed crackles, radar lock beeps, brass-stab fanfares). A sound can instead be `{ kind: 'sample', file, duration }` pointing at a file in the pack's `assets/` or `data/audio/`.
- **Music slot:** a pack may export one `music` (`MusicDef`: a looping sample file, or a synthesised loop of up to 64 eighth-note steps plus an optional bass line, with its own volume). It starts when the audio unlocks and loops; the mix's Music volume scales it, and sounds with `duck` dip it for a moment (big explosions, radio and pilot sounds). `music.ts` is the pure part (steps to timed notes); `webaudio.ts` schedules them a second ahead. A pack's index passes `music` on next to `sounds`.
- **Richer synthesis (additive fields, all optional, old packs stay valid):** a layer can `hold` at full level before it decays, `detune` (cents), `distortion` (waveshaper drive), `tremolo` (rate, depth), use `pink` or `brown` noise, and a sound may have up to 12 layers. A sound entry can add `reverb` (send to the shared space reverb) and `preDelay`, `duckLoops` (dip the continuous loops), and `spatial` gains `lowpass` (cutoff near and far: distance muffles), `farReverb` (far sounds are wetter) and `lag` (far sounds arrive late). The reverb is a `ConvolverNode` on a generated impulse response (noise that decays and darkens); its level and length are mix parameters (`reverb`, `reverbTime`). Everything ends in a limiter and a soft ceiling (`OUTPUT_CEILING` 0.9).
- **Loops (continuous sounds):** a pack's `loops` table (`engine`, `afterburner`, `rumble`, `ambient`, `missiles`, `rescue`, `hullAlarm`, `edgeAlarm`; each an entry or `'silent'`). An entry is a few steady layers plus curves from a game value (`speed`, `throttle`, `hull`, `rescue`, `missiles`, `edge`, `always`) to loudness (`gain`), pitch and filter cutoff, with `fadeIn` and `fadeOut` seconds. `state.ts` (`loopStateOf(world)`) reads the world into those values (all 0 outside a battle); `loops.ts` (pure) samples the curves, smooths the loudness and says which loops are on; `webaudio.ts` keeps persistent nodes per loop and only sets their values. The app calls `engine.setLoopState(...)` once per frame. The panel's Sound section edits a loop's volume, fades and reverb, and previews a loop at a chosen value.
- **Events in:** every `GameEvent` (by its type) plus the app's `Paused` and `Resumed`; each has a sound or an explicit `'silent'` in every pack (a pack whose parent is `plain` (or none) takes the default sound pack's entry for events and loops it leaves out, see `SOUND_DEFAULT_STYLE`). Events added for sound: `EnemyShotFired`, `WingmanShotFired`, `PlayerDamaged`, `WingmanHit`, `WingmanDown`, `MissileImpact`, `ArenaEdgeEntered/Left`, `PlayerRespawned`, `MenuMove/Select/Back/Tick/Pick`. Events are not state: the replay hash is unchanged.
- **Test:** `engine.test.ts` (fake backend: bounds, gaps, voices, pan, size, duck, pause, mute), `space.test.ts` (distance cues, curves), `loops.test.ts` (curves, fades, loop state), `tests/sim/audio.sim.test.ts` (hash with audio on or off, in practice and in a run), e2e (M key, Sound section).
