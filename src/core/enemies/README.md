# core/enemies

- **Purpose:** the contract for Prototype 5 (`docs/specs/prototype-5-enemy-variety.md`): enemy kinds, weapon mounts, formation wings, enemy missiles, the capital ship's parts and the authored battle table, as **types, validators and empty inert state**. No behaviour lives here yet; tracks A, B and C build on it (`docs/p5-tracks.md`).
- **Files:** `kinds.ts` (`EnemyKind`, ids, `validateEnemyKind(s)`), `mounts.ts` (`WeaponMount`, `validateMount`), `capital-parts.ts` (`CapitalPartDef`, `PartRole`, `validateCapitalParts`), `battles.ts` (`BattleTable`, `validateBattleTable`), `state.ts` (`EnemyState`: enemy missile pool, `WingState[]`, `CapitalState | null`; `createEnemyState`, `clearEnemyState`, `mixEnemies`), `validate.ts` (check helpers).
- **Data:** `data/content/enemies.ts` lists the kinds (one file each in `data/content/kinds/`), `data/content/capital.ts` the parts (empty until track C), `data/content/battles.ts` the ramp (today's behaviour for now), `data/tuning/{gunship,lancer,capital,wings}.ts` parameter stubs (not yet in `Tuning`).
- **State:** `world.enemies` (`missiles`, `wings`, `capital`), empty today. Cleared by `resetWorld` and the run's `clearField`.
- **Hash:** `mixEnemies` writes nothing while the state is empty, so existing hashes are unchanged. Anything you add to the state types goes into `mixEnemies` and into the audit in `core/replay/hash-coverage.test.ts`.
- **Events:** `EnemySpawned`, `EnemyMissileFired`, `EnemyMissileHit`, `PartDestroyed`, `CoreExposed`, `WingBroken`, `CapitalDestroyed` are declared (see `core/events`); the capital ship's three are emitted (see below), the others by their tracks.
- **Units:** world units (u), seconds (s), radians. Mount and part positions are in ship space (x forward, y left).
- **Test:** `enemies.test.ts` (good and bad kinds, mounts, parts, battle tables), `data/content/battles.test.ts` (the default table equals today's waves; the fighter kind equals the fighter tuning), `core/replay/hash-coverage.test.ts`.

## The capital ship (Prototype 5, track C)

- **Purpose:** the boss of battle 4 (spec section 5): a huge ship made of independent parts, won by destroying its **core**. Parts are data (`data/content/capital.ts`: 6 rapid-fire turrets, 2 heavy turrets, 2 engines, 5 armour plates, a bridge, a core; checked by `validateCapitalParts` at load and by a test over the real list), its numbers are the `capital` tuning group (`data/tuning/capital.ts`, in the panel's "Capital ship" section).
- **Files:** `capital.ts` (building the state, part geometry, shielding, `damagePart`, the lockable bodies), `capital-hits.ts` (which part a bullet touches: broad phase by hull radius, then the shallowest overlap, so the outermost part takes it), `capital-ai.ts` (drift and broadside turn, turrets, the death chain), `capital-battle.ts` (battle 4: spawn at the arena edge, escort timing, victory), `escorts.ts` (the two escort hooks, see below), `capital-hud.ts` (read-only health bar data). Drawing: `render/capital.ts`, `render/hud/capital-bar.ts`, `dev/capital-overlay.ts`.
- **State read/written:** `world.enemies.capital` (`CapitalState`: position, heading, velocity, `parts[]` with `hp`, `maxHp`, `alive`, turret `cooldown` and `burstLeft`, `coreExposed`, `phase`, timers). Part positions are derived from the ship pose on demand (ship space x forward, y left, scaled by `hullRadius / CAPITAL_DESIGN_RADIUS`), never stored. All of it is in the replay hash.
- **Damage routing:** player and wingman bullets are tested after `stepBullets` (`stepCapitalBullets`), missiles through their lockable id (`damagePart`). One rule, in `damagePart`: **a part covered by a standing plate cannot be damaged, hit or locked** (the core is covered by four plates, the bridge by the bow plate). `CoreExposed` is emitted once, when the last plate covering the core dies.
- **Movement:** drift towards the player at `cruiseSpeed` until `standoff`, turn the broadside to them at `turnRate`, both scaled by the share of engines still alive (half the engines = half speed and turn, none = a sitting hulk that stops). The whole hull stays inside the arena (`keepInside`).
- **Guns:** each turret fires from its mount inside its arc and range, at the nearest of the player, wingmen and pods, with lead; bursts of `burst.shots` with a `burst.pause` (a window). A dead bridge: no lead, extra aim error (`blindSpread`, less with `blindAccuracyScale`) and a longer pause (`blindReaction`). Shots go into the shared enemy shot pool (cap `arena.enemyShotCap`, now 160); a heavy round is `bulletDamage` bullets in a row (enemy bullets all do 1 hull).
- **Death:** the core's death starts a chain (`phase` 1): the remaining parts blow up one after another over most of `deathChainTime` (turrets, engines, plates, bridge), then `CapitalDestroyed` ends it (`phase` 2) and the battle is won. Each `PartDestroyed{role, radius}` and `CapitalDestroyed{radius}` is drawn by the style's death data (`ships`/`deaths` kinds `capital`, `capitalTurret`, `capitalEngine`, `capitalArmour`, `capitalBridge`, `capitalCore`; placeholders in `plain`).
- **Battle 4 script:** `data/content/battles.ts` row 4 has `boss: 'capital'`; `startBattle` spawns the ship, `stepRunBattle` calls `stepCapitalBattle` instead of the waves. Escort wings arrive on a timer (the first with the ship, then every `escortWingDelay`), the missile fighters when half the approach is done or after `lancerFallbackTime`. **Both escort kinds go through `escorts.ts` (`escortHooks.sendWing`, `sendLancers`): until track A's formation wings and track B's lancers are on the branch they are plain fighters; replace those two function bodies when they land.**
- **Lock-on and orders:** lockable ids from `PART_ID_BASE` (2000) up are parts (`part index + 2000`; targets stay below 1000, fighters 1000 to 1999). Only parts that can be hit are lockable, and a lock set holds at most `lockPartCap` parts. Attack orders and wingmen can target parts (nearest turret first); when an ordered part dies the order moves on to the nearest turret, then the nearest part.
- **Dev:** spawn entry "Capital ship" (one at a time), Jump: Battle 4, "Next wave" in battle 4 sends the next escort wing, "Clear battle" kills the core (the chain then runs), debug overlay (G) shows part circles, hp, shield links and arcs.
- **Test:** `capital.test.ts` (the real data, geometry, damage routing, plate shielding, `CoreExposed`, engines, bridge, arcs, bursts, the chain), `render/hud/capital-bar.test.ts`, `tests/sim/capital.sim.test.ts` (scripted bots kill the parts in different orders: ends, no NaN, bounded pools, deterministic, battle 4 through the run flow), `tests/sim/capital-cost.sim.test.ts` (cost), `tests/e2e/capital.spec.ts`.
