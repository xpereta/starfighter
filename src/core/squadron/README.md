# core/squadron

- **Purpose:** the wingmen, formations (tight / spread) and orders (attack my target) (spec sections 4 and 5).
- **Status:** wingmen are implemented (issue B2). Orders are issue B3; wingmen launching the salvo is integration issue I1.
- **Files:** `squadron.ts` (state, `livingWingmen`, `stepSquadron`, `mixSquadron`), `formation.ts` (pure slot math), `wingmen.ts` (creation, AI, guns, damage, respawn).
- **Contract:** `world.squadron.wingmen` (each with a `ship`, `hp`, `alive`, plus AI state), `formation`, `order`, `orderTimer`. `livingWingmen(squadron)` is the number of extra pilots: lock-on uses it for the lock limit and missiles for the salvo size. Wingmen are friends and never lockable. `stepSquadron(world)` runs after the fighters and before lock-on. Orders are edge-triggered: read `actions.cycleFormation && !world.prev.cycleFormation` (prev is updated at the end of the step). Events: `OrderGiven`; `Killed{kind: 'wingman', entityId: index}`. Anything you add must also go into `mixSquadron` (replay hash).
- **Creation:** wingmen are created from `wingmanCount` (default 2, 0-4) on their formation slots at the player's velocity; changing it live grows or shrinks the list, and a respawn clears it so the next step re-creates them.
- **Flying:** wingmen use the player's flight model through AI actions (always point-to-steer), never their own flight maths. Without an enemy to engage a wingman flies to its slot (the formation point moved ahead by `slotLeadTime` of the player's velocity), matches the player's heading once within `slotHoldRadius`, and matches speed (throttle from how far behind the slot it is plus the speed gap). Slots: **tight** = behind the player on alternating sides (index 0 left), further pairs further back and out, sized by `tightRadius`; **spread** = a ring of `spreadRadius` at fixed world angles. Separation: inside `separation` of the player or another living wingman it adds a push away (`separationGain`) to its heading vector. A hard spin can make the tight slots swing faster than ships can follow, so wingmen may cross paths briefly, but they re-form and never stay stacked.
- **Fighting:** each wingman engages the nearest fighter, drone or turret (not static targets) and keeps it until it is more than 25% beyond range. **Tight** only engages enemies within `tightEngageRange` of the _player_; **spread** engages anything within `spreadEngageRange` of the _wingman_; both fly at a lead point and return to the slot when nothing is left. It shoots inside `fireCone` and `fireRange`, with bullets in the player's bullet pool but per-bullet damage `gunDamage` (lower than the player's, so aim still matters). Friendly bullets never hurt friends (they are only tested against targets and fighters).
- **Losses:** enemy bullets (`world.enemyShots`) that reach a wingman take 1 hp each and emit `Hit`; at 0 hp it emits `Killed{kind: 'wingman'}`, stops flying, and returns after `respawnDelay` (test arena only; a real run would lose the pilot).
- **Parameters:** `data/tuning/squadron.ts` (Wingmen section of the panel).
- **Test:** `formation.test.ts`, `wingmen.test.ts`; `tests/sim/wingmen.sim.test.ts` (120 s with 4 wingmen and waves; the default 2-wingman run replays bit-exactly).
