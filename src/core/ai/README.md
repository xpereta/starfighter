# core/ai

- **Purpose:** enemy fighters and their waves (spec section 3). A fighter flies with the **same flight model as the player** (`stepFlight`): the AI only writes its `actions`, using its own flight config derived from the player's (`deriveFlight`: turn rate x `turnRateScale`, speeds x `speedScale`, point steering).
- **Files:** `fighter.ts` (the `Fighter` state and `mixFighters`), `steering.ts` (derived flight config, `leadPoint`), `fighters.ts` (`stepFighters`, targeting), `waves.ts` (kills, wave spawning, `stepWaves`).
- **Contract:** a `Fighter` is a `Collider` (bullets and missiles can hit it) with a velocity kept in sync with its `ship`; `world.fighters[i]` has lockable id `FIGHTER_ID_BASE + i` (`core/world/lockable.ts`). Dead fighters stay in the array with `alive = false` so ids stay stable; the next wave reuses dead slots first. `stepFighters(world)` runs after the player's flight, `stepWaves(world)` after enemy shots. Emits `Killed{kind: 'fighter', entityId: FIGHTER_ID_BASE + i}` and counts kills in `world.stats`. Anything you add to `Fighter` must also go into `mixFighters` (replay hash).
- **Behaviour per step:** keep or re-pick the target (the nearest of the player and living wingmen, every `retargetInterval`); fly at a lead point (`leadPoint`: where a bullet fired now meets the target); close in beyond `fireRange`, ease off inside `minRange`, brake to corner speed when the target is far off the nose; shoot when the lead point is inside `fireCone` and `fireRange` (slower bullets into `world.enemyShots`, which hit the player); **break away** for `breakTime` (hard turn `breakAngle` off the target line, flat out, with an evade roll) after two hits within `breakHitWindow` or when the player has it locked, then wait `breakCooldown` before it may break again. In its evade i-frames a fighter is `immune` to bullets.
- **Missile evasion** (`missile-evasion.ts` pure helpers, `evadeMissiles` in `fighters.ts`; switch `fighter.enemiesEvadeMissiles`): each step a fighter looks for the nearest missile homing on it (`targetId` = its lockable id) inside `missileDetectRange`. The first time it sees a given missile (`uid`) it rolls once whether it notices (`missileEvadeChance`, reduced by `missileFarPenalty` towards the range edge) and draws a reaction error (+-`missileReactionError`). If it noticed, it rolls (evade, edge-triggered through the flight model) when the estimated time to impact (distance over closing speed) drops to `evadeIFrames / 2 + error`, so the missile arrives inside its i-frames when the timing is good and outside them when the error is big. One attempt per missile; the roll needs `missileEvadeCooldown` (its own, the break-away roll does not use it up) and no roll in progress, so a salvo of several missiles at one fighter still lands most of them (a roll's i-frames also absorb missiles arriving within them). Fighter state: `missileCooldown`, `missileUid`, `missilePlan` (0 ignore, 1 will react, 2 done), `missileTrigger`; all in `mixFighters`, randomness from `world.rng`. Missiles skip immune bodies (see `core/weapons`): a missile that touches its immune target loses its lock and flies on straight. Drones and turrets never evade. No event is emitted.
- **Waves:** `stepWaves` first resolves kills, then spawns `waveSize` fighters around the arena edge (`spawnFraction` of the arena radius, heading inward) as soon as none are alive and `waveDelay` has passed since the last death (at once if there never was a wave). `waveSize` 0 turns waves off. Wave state is `world.fighters` itself (`diedAt`), so a respawn that clears it restarts the waves.
- **Step length:** subsystem hooks are not handed `dt`; `stepSeconds(world)` (`core/world/clock.ts`) recovers it from the clock.
- **Parameters:** `data/tuning/fighter.ts` (Enemy fighter section of the panel).
- **Test:** `fighters.test.ts`, `waves.test.ts`, `steering.test.ts`; `tests/sim/fighters.sim.test.ts` (120 s waves: finite, in the arena, pools within caps, replay matches).
- **Battle waves (prototype 5):** `spawnBattleWave(world, wave)` spawns the groups of one battle-table wave in order; `spawnGroup` handles one group (fighters like a classic wave, so today's table is bit-identical; kinds without a spawner yet are skipped). `spawnFighter` emits `EnemySpawned`.
- **Gunship (prototype 5, spec section 3):** `gunship.ts` + `mount-aim.ts` (pure: arcs, slewing, burst windows). A gunship is a `Fighter` entry with `shipType = SHIP_GUNSHIP` (so ids, locks, kill credit, wave clearing and wingman targeting all work unchanged) and `mounts` (one `MountState` per kind mount: aim, cooldown, burst, pause; all in `mixFighters`). `spawnGunship` reads `world.tuning.gunship` (hull, radius, speed and turn scale) and the kind's mounts (`data/content/kinds/gunship.ts`), whose live values (arc, rate, burst, bullet, range) come from the `gunship` tuning (`tunedMount`). It flies the player's flight model scaled by `speedScale`/`turnRateScale` (never below the flight minimum speed). AI: keep `standoff` from the player (closer than `standoff - band` it backs off, further than `standoff + band` it closes, between it circles broadside on its seeded side; a player missile homing on it makes it back off); each turret is independent: it picks the player if in arc and range, otherwise the nearest wingman in arc and range, swings at `turretTurnRate` inside its arc (aims with a partial lead), fires in bursts when within `turretFireCone` of the aim. Bullets go into `world.enemyShots` (1 hull each, like every enemy shot: the pool has no damage field, so `bulletDamage` of a mount is reserved), `EnemyShotFired{from: 'gunship'}`. Kill = `Killed{kind: 'gunship'}`. The two arcs leave a thin blind wedge straight behind (the flank).
- **Formation wings (prototype 5, spec section 2):** `wings.ts` (pure slot geometry `slotOffset`/`slotWorld`, the break rules `breakReason`, `stepWings`, `spawnWing`/`spawnWings`, `wingCue`). A wing (`world.enemies.wings[i]`: shape v/line/box, leader index, follower indices in slot order, `broken`, `born`) is ordinary `world.fighters` entries tagged with `wingId` and `wingSlot`; the leader (hull + `leaderExtraHp`) flies the normal fighter AI (but does not break away when locked while the wing holds), followers (`thinkFollower` in `fighters.ts`) use the squadron's slot logic (`slotHoldRadius`, `slotLeadTime`, catch-up boost) around the leader, point at the leader's target once it is in range and fire together. Break rules (checked each step by `stepWings`, `WingBroken{reason}` once): leader dead -> `leader`, any member below its max hull -> `fire`, the player within `breakProximity` of any member -> `proximity`; then every living member retargets at once and flies as an ordinary fighter. A member slot that a later spawn reused (its `wingId` differs) is ignored. Cue: `wingCue(world)` returns `WING INBOUND` (`data/content/cues.ts`) for `wings.cueTime` seconds after a wing arrives while it holds formation; the HUD shows it through `ui/cues.ts`. Wings come from the battle table (`kind: 'wing'`, `count` = wings, `wings.size` fighters each) and from the dev spawn registry. Parameters: `data/tuning/wings.ts` (and the `wings.shape` toggle to force a formation).
- **Prototype 5:** enemy kinds, mounts, wings and the battle table are specified as data and types in `core/enemies` (README there); the existing fighter is the `fighter` kind (`data/content/kinds/fighter.ts`) and still runs on `tuning.fighter`. Who builds what: `docs/p5-tracks.md`.

## Missile fighter ("lancer", prototype 5 track B)

- A lancer is a `Fighter` with `lancer` set (`lancer-state.ts`: `missileTimer`, `burstLeft`, `burstTimer`, `side`), in `world.fighters` like any fighter (kill credit, `Killed{kind: 'fighter'}`, waves count it, lock-on and missiles hit it, it dodges the player's missiles with the same `evadeMissiles`). Its flight model is the fighter's with `tuning.lancer.speedScale` and `turnRateScale` (faster than a fighter); hull and radius come from `data/content/kinds/lancer.ts`.
- **`think`** runs the fighter's break-away and missile evasion unchanged, then hands over to `thinkLancer` (`lancer.ts`): it always targets the PLAYER (never a wingman), keeps `rangeMin`..`rangeMax` (`lancerMove`: pursue beyond, flee inside, circle in the band, point its nose at the player when a missile is due), and launches (`launchEnemyMissile`) when due, in the band, nose within `launchCone` and not evading (`canLaunch`; a break-away or a roll drops the rest of a burst). One launch per `missileInterval`, the first one staggered by the seeded rng; a burst of `burstSize` (`burstSizeFor`: only in runs from `burstFromBattle`) leaves `burstGap` apart. It has no guns. Emits `EnemyMissileFired`.
- **Spawning:** `spawnLancer` (dev panel "Spawn Missile fighter") and `convertWaveToLancers`, which in run battles turns `LANCER_RAMP[battle][wave]` fighters of the freshly spawned wave into lancers in place. It is a local hook behind `tuning.lancer.inBattles` (default off) until the authored battle table spawns kinds.
