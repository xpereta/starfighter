# core/ai

- **Purpose:** enemy fighters and their waves (spec section 3). A fighter flies with the **same flight model as the player** (`stepFlight`): the AI only writes its `actions`, using its own flight config derived from the player's (`deriveFlight`: turn rate x `turnRateScale`, speeds x `speedScale`, point steering).
- **Files:** `fighter.ts` (the `Fighter` state and `mixFighters`), `steering.ts` (derived flight config, `leadPoint`), `fighters.ts` (`stepFighters`, targeting), `waves.ts` (kills, wave spawning, `stepWaves`).
- **Contract:** a `Fighter` is a `Collider` (bullets and missiles can hit it) with a velocity kept in sync with its `ship`; `world.fighters[i]` has lockable id `FIGHTER_ID_BASE + i` (`core/world/lockable.ts`). Dead fighters stay in the array with `alive = false` so ids stay stable; the next wave reuses dead slots first. `stepFighters(world)` runs after the player's flight, `stepWaves(world)` after enemy shots. Emits `Killed{kind: 'fighter', entityId: FIGHTER_ID_BASE + i}` and counts kills in `world.stats`. Anything you add to `Fighter` must also go into `mixFighters` (replay hash).
- **Behaviour per step:** keep or re-pick the target (the nearest of the player and living wingmen, every `retargetInterval`); fly at a lead point (`leadPoint`: where a bullet fired now meets the target); close in beyond `fireRange`, ease off inside `minRange`, brake to corner speed when the target is far off the nose; shoot when the lead point is inside `fireCone` and `fireRange` (slower bullets into `world.enemyShots`, which hit the player); **break away** for `breakTime` (hard turn `breakAngle` off the target line, flat out, with an evade roll) after two hits within `breakHitWindow` or when the player has it locked, then wait `breakCooldown` before it may break again. In its evade i-frames a fighter is `immune` to bullets.
- **Waves:** `stepWaves` first resolves kills, then spawns `waveSize` fighters around the arena edge (`spawnFraction` of the arena radius, heading inward) as soon as none are alive and `waveDelay` has passed since the last death (at once if there never was a wave). `waveSize` 0 turns waves off. Wave state is `world.fighters` itself (`diedAt`), so a respawn that clears it restarts the waves.
- **Step length:** subsystem hooks are not handed `dt`; `stepSeconds(world)` (`core/world/clock.ts`) recovers it from the clock.
- **Parameters:** `data/tuning/fighter.ts` (Enemy fighter section of the panel).
- **Test:** `fighters.test.ts`, `waves.test.ts`, `steering.test.ts`; `tests/sim/fighters.sim.test.ts` (120 s waves: finite, in the arena, pools within caps, replay matches).
